About Sonaxia

What is Sonaxia?

Sonaxia is an AI-powered study app for students and teachers. It turns recorded lectures into clean notes, quizzes, flashcards and mind maps, answers study doubts through Home AI, and helps you prepare for exams — all at a low cost, starting around ₹25.

Who is Sonaxia for?

Students preparing for school exams and competitive/government exams, and teachers who want to record lectures, share notes with their students, and run paid study Groups. Sonaxia is built primarily for learners across India, Bangladesh, and South Asia.

What makes Sonaxia different from other study apps?

Sonaxia combines lecture recording, AI notes, doubt-solving, exam-specific question banks, and language learning in one low-cost app — instead of needing five different apps. Pricing is built to be far cheaper than typical AI/tutoring subscriptions.

Recording & Study Materials

Can I record a lecture and get notes automatically?

Yes. Record audio (or upload an existing recording), and Sonaxia transcribes it and generates clean, organized study notes automatically.

Does Sonaxia generate quizzes from my notes?

Yes. From any recorded lecture or uploaded document, Sonaxia can generate quizzes, flashcards, revision sheets, and mind maps to help you revise faster.

Can I upload a PDF or photo of notes instead of recording?

Yes. You can upload PDFs or photos of handwritten/printed notes, and Sonaxia will process them into structured notes and study material the same way.

Can I make a mind map from my lecture?

Yes. Mind Map is one of the study tools available for any processed lecture or Home AI answer, helping you see how concepts connect.

Home AI (Doubt Solving)

What is Home AI?

Home AI is Sonaxia's study-doubt chat — ask any exam-related question, and it answers using your own notes first, then trusted knowledge, giving clear, exam-focused explanations.

Can Home AI help with any subject, not just recorded lectures?

Yes. Home AI answers general syllabus and exam-prep questions even without an open lecture — maths, science, history, reasoning, aptitude, and more.

Does Sonaxia show previous year questions (PYQs)?

Yes. Where available, Sonaxia references previous year question patterns and generates fresh practice questions in that style to help you prepare.

Exam Preparation

Which exams does Sonaxia help prepare for?

Sonaxia supports preparation for school boards (CBSE and others), and major competitive/government exams including NEET, Railway, Bank exams, and other government job exams.

Is Sonaxia good for competitive government exam preparation?

Yes. Home AI, generated quizzes, important-questions, and revision sheets are built to support focused, exam-style preparation for competitive exams at a fraction of typical coaching or tutoring cost.

Pricing & Credits

How much does Sonaxia cost?

Sonaxia uses an affordable credit-based system with plans starting from around ₹25 for a top-up, and low-cost monthly plans well below typical AI tutoring subscription prices.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. A free plan is available with a starting credit balance so you can try Ask AI, PDF/photo analysis, and other features before upgrading.

Why is Sonaxia cheaper than other AI study tools?

Sonaxia is built to be accessible for students across South Asia, so pricing is kept deliberately low compared to Western AI subscription pricing, while still covering real study needs.

Groups & Teachers

What are Sonaxia Groups?

Groups are teacher-led study channels. Students join a teacher's Group to receive shared lectures, notes, quizzes, and announcements from that teacher.

Can students join multiple teachers' Groups?

Yes, depending on your plan. Higher plans allow joining more Groups, so students can learn from multiple teachers at once.

Can teachers earn money on Sonaxia?

Teachers can create their own Groups and share paid lecture content with students. A teacher earning/commission system is in active development.

How do teachers record and share lectures?

Teachers use the same recording tools as students, then share generated notes, quizzes, or full lectures directly into their Group's feed for members to study from.

Progress & Practice

Can I track my daily study progress?

Yes. Sonaxia includes a Progress section so students can see study activity and stay consistent day to day.

Does Sonaxia offer AI interview practice?

AI-based interview practice is part of Sonaxia's roadmap, aimed at helping students prepare for interviews alongside written exams.

Language Learning

Can I learn English on Sonaxia?

Yes. You can ask Home AI to teach you English, and it will explain in your own conversation language (Hindi, Bengali, or others) so learning stays easy to follow.

Can I learn other languages too?

Yes. Home AI can help you learn most languages, not just English — just ask it to teach you the language you want.

Do I need to know English to use Sonaxia?

No. Sonaxia's Home AI responds in the same language you ask in — Hindi, Bengali, Hinglish, English, and more — so you can study in your native language.
